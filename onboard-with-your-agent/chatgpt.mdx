---
title: "ChatGPT"
description: "Connect Jam to ChatGPT as a custom connector with OAuth sign-in."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode">
    Custom connectors need ChatGPT's developer mode. Turn it on in ChatGPT's settings, under connectors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    Create a connector with:

    | Field | Value |
    | --- | --- |
    | URL | `https://api.spreadjam.com/mcp` |
    | Authentication | OAuth |

  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Jam">
    ChatGPT opens a Jam page. Sign in (or sign up and choose a plan), check the workspace and where you will be sent back to, and approve.
  </Step>
</Steps>

ChatGPT registers itself with Jam on the first sign-in, so there is no client id or secret to enter.

## Check it works

In a new chat with the connector on, ask: "Use Jam: which workspace are you connected to, and what is our visibility score?"

## Disconnect

The connection appears in Jam under **Settings**, **Developer** as a key named after the client. Revoke it there.

<Note>
  ChatGPT's menus for connectors change often and differ by plan. If the steps
  above do not match what you see, the two values that matter are the URL and
  OAuth authentication.
</Note>
