title: "The Plan"
description: "The Plan is the ranked list of actions that should raise your AI visibility: pages to write or fix, technical changes, sources to earn and people to pitch."
---

Research writes the Plan. Each action says what to do, why (the evidence from the answers and sources), which tracked prompts it should move and which page it targets.

## Kinds of action

| Kind                   | What it is                                                   | Who can do it                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `new_page` | A page to publish, such as a comparison or a guide. | Jam drafts it, or your agent writes it. |
| `page_edit` | A change to a page already on your site. | Jam prepares the code change, or your agent makes it. |
| `technical` | A technical fix on your site. | Jam prepares the code change, or your agent makes it. |
| `outreach` | Pitch a third-party list or page. | Your agent or you, from `agentPrompt`. A Send to Jam run can draft it. |
| `community` | Take part in a community thread. | Your agent or you. A Send to Jam run can draft it. |

## Status

| Status                    | Meaning                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `suggested`               | On the Plan, not started.                                        |
| `in_progress`             | Someone started it.                                              |
| `sent`                    | Outreach went out; waiting on the third party.                   |
| `done`                    | Reported done.                                                   |
| `verified` / `unverified` | Jam checked whether it took effect.                              |
| `dismissed`               | Taken off the Plan.                                              |
| `superseded`              | Research no longer proposes it. Hidden from the list by default. |

Transitions are safe to repeat: starting a started action, or dismissing a dismissed one, changes nothing. To take a started action off the Plan, dismiss it; restoring it puts it back as `suggested`.

## Read it from your agent

- `list_actions`: the Plan, best first, with `countsByStatus` covering every page.
- `get_action`: everything for one action, including `agentPrompt`, the instructions for your agent.

Actions and opportunities are two lists over one Plan. Opportunities (`list_opportunities`) are the article pipeline behind `new_page` actions: when Jam drafts one, the action carries its `opportunityId`. Work from actions; read an opportunity only to write the article yourself from its brief.
