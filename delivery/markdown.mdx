---
title: "Markdown"
description: "Get each article as Markdown to publish yourself, or hand it to your coding agent to add to your site."
---

With Markdown delivery, Jam writes and stores the article and you publish it. Choose **Use Markdown** on the Plan, or switch in **Settings**.

## Get the article

Open a drafted article and use:

- **Download Markdown**: a `.md` file of the current revision.
- **Copy Markdown**: the same text on your clipboard.
- **Copy prompt**: instructions for your coding agent to add the page to your site.

Over the API, the text is at `currentRevision.article.bodyMarkdown` on `get_content` (`GET /v1/content/{contentId}`). The list view (`list_content`) carries the title and state, not the body.

## Tell Jam where it is live

After you publish, record the URL with **Mark published**, or `mark_content_published` with `{"url": "https://acme.com/blog/..."}`. The page then shows as live.

## Write it with your own agent

Your agent can write the article instead of Jam:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the brief">
    `get_opportunity_brief`. The first read plans the article and can take 30 to
    60 seconds; it is included in the plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim it">
    `claim_opportunity`, so Jam and other agents do not write it at the same
    time. It answers `409` when someone else holds it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store the draft">
    `create_content` with the article: `title`, `metaDescription`, `slug`, `keywords` and `bodyMarkdown`. This releases the claim. If
    the agent cannot finish, `report_opportunity_agent_failure` with a reason,
    or `release_opportunity_claim`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The draft then follows the same delivery as one Jam wrote.
