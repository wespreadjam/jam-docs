## 1. Set up your site

Open [spreadjam.com/start](https://www.spreadjam.com/start) and sign in or create an account. Setup asks a few questions, each on its own page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="What site should Jam track?">
    Enter your website, for example `acme.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="How should Jam describe you?">
    Jam drafts a description of your company from your site. Edit anything that
    is off: this is how Jam talks about you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Who buys from you, and who do you compete with?">
    Your audiences and competitors. Jam uses them to write prompts and to rank
    you against the right brands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Which questions should Jam watch?">
    The prompts: questions buyers ask AI assistants when they look for something
    like you. Edit, add or remove any. See [Prompts](/tracking/prompts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan">
    Pick a prompt capacity, a scan cadence, the platforms and your work credits.
    A new account gets a 14-day free trial; checkout asks for a card. See [Plans
    and credits](/billing/plans-and-credits).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Read your first results

The first scan starts when your plan is active. It asks every prompt on every platform, so it takes a few minutes; the numbers fill in as answers arrive. You then see:

- **Visibility**: how often AI assistants mention and recommend you, against your competitors.
- **Answers**: the full text of every AI answer, with the brands it named and the URLs it cited.
- **Sources**: which sites the assistants cite. Your own site is marked.

See [Scans and answers](/tracking/scans-and-answers).

## 3. Work the Plan

When the first scan finishes, Jam runs its first [research](/research/overview) (free) and writes the Plan. Jam also drafts up to three of the top new pages by itself, once, from your plan's credits.

Each action on the Plan has two buttons: **Send to Jam** has Jam do it, and **Copy prompt** hands it to your own agent. See [Doing the work](/actions/doing-the-work).

## 4. Choose how pages reach your site

The Plan asks once: **Use Markdown** (you get each article as a file to publish yourself) or **Connect GitHub** (Jam opens a pull request on your site's repository). You can change it later in **Settings**. See [Delivery](/delivery/overview).
