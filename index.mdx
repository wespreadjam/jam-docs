Jam does GEO (generative engine optimization): it asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot the questions your buyers ask, records every answer, and shows whether your brand is mentioned, recommended and cited. The gaps become a Plan of actions: pages to write or fix, sources to earn, competitors to answer.

Everything a customer does in the Jam dashboard is open to an agent:

- **MCP server** at `https://api.spreadjam.com/mcp`, one tool per API route. Claude.ai, Claude Desktop and ChatGPT sign in with OAuth; other clients send an API key.
- **REST API** at `https://api.spreadjam.com/v1`, described by the [OpenAPI spec](https://api.spreadjam.com/openapi.json).
- **Agent connect**: an agent with no key sends its human one link, and gets a key once they approve.

## What an agent can do

- Read visibility: the overview, per-prompt numbers, the brand leaderboard, platform health and the raw AI answers.
- Manage what is tracked: add, reword, pause or remove prompts, and edit the brand and its competitors.
- Work the Plan: list actions, start them, draft content, open pull requests on your site, and mark them done.
- Run scans and research when the numbers need refreshing.
- Get notified: send Jam's notifications to your own URL with [webhooks](/agents/webhooks).

Work that writes or opens pull requests spends credits. Every tool that does says so at the start of its description, and `GET /v1/credits` shows the balance.
