Jam asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot (and Claude, as an add-on) the questions your buyers ask. It records every answer and shows whether your brand is mentioned, recommended and cited. Research turns the gaps into a Plan: pages to write or fix, technical changes, sources to earn. Jam can do the work and ship it to your site, or hand it to your own agent.

## How Jam works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tracking">
    Jam asks every [tracked prompt](/tracking/prompts) on every platform your
    plan covers, on your plan's cadence, and keeps each answer. See
    [Tracking](/tracking/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Research">
    Jam reads the answers to learn which pages AI assistants trust, which
    sources cite your competitors and where you are missing. See
    [Research](/research/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Actions">
    Research becomes the Plan: a ranked list of actions, each with its evidence.
    Send one to Jam, copy its prompt into your own agent, or do it yourself. See
    [The Plan](/actions/the-plan).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery">
    Finished pages reach your site as a pull request on GitHub, or as Markdown
    you publish yourself. See [Delivery](/delivery/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two ways to work

- **The dashboard** at [spreadjam.com](https://www.spreadjam.com/start). Everything in these docs has a button there.
- **Your own agent.** The [MCP server](/api-reference/mcp) at `https://api.spreadjam.com/mcp` and the [REST API](/api-reference/overview) at `https://api.spreadjam.com/v1` expose everything the dashboard does. Claude and ChatGPT sign in with OAuth; other clients send an API key.

<Note>
  Work that writes pages, opens pull requests or runs research again spends
  credits. Scans of your tracked prompts do not. See [Plans and
  credits](/billing/plans-and-credits).
</Note>
